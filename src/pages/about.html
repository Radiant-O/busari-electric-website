Our vision

A world where charging an electric car is as easy as filling up a tank: close by, open when you need it, and simple to pay for.

Our mission

To make charging easy for every EV driver. We bring reliable fast chargers to our community with the partners who own them, keep them open day and night, and help drivers who want to charge at home. Arlington is where we start.

Visiting the station

Where is the BusterElectric charging station?{{icon:chevron-down}}

At 2404 South Collins Street, Arlington, Texas 76014. Use Google Maps directions or see it on our map.

Is the station open 24 hours?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Yes. The chargers are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Can a Tesla charge here?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Yes. The fast chargers have NACS (Tesla) connectors, so a Tesla plugs straight in. Cars with a CCS1 port, such as Ford, Chevrolet, Hyundai, Kia, BMW and Volkswagen, use the CCS1 connector.

Who runs the chargers?{{icon:chevron-down}}

The chargers are on the {{partner.name}} network. BusterElectric is the local outlet that partners with {{partner.name}} to run this station. You start and pay for charging through {{partner.name}}, with their app or a card.

Do you install home chargers?{{icon:chevron-down}}

We arrange Level 1 and Level 2 charger installation for homes. Send a request and we will contact you.
