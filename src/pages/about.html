Charging that works the first time

Busari Electric builds and runs public EV charging stations in the United States. Our goal is simple: when you pull in, a charger is free, it works, and you know the price.

What we do

We find sites along the routes people drive, install DC fast and Level 2 chargers, and operate them on the Busari Electric network. We also install and run chargers for businesses, property owners and fleets.

Every station we run is monitored remotely. When a charger has a problem, we can often fix it remotely. When it needs hands-on repair, we send a technician.
