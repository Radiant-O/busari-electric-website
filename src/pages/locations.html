    Using the station map

    What does "available" mean on the map?{{icon:chevron-down}}

    Available means a plug is free and ready to charge right now. Counts update about every minute. A plug can be taken between the time you look and the time you arrive, so busy stations show how many plugs they have in total.

    Which plug does my car need?{{icon:chevron-down}}

    Most non-Tesla EVs sold in the US use CCS1 for fast charging. Tesla cars and a growing number of 2025 and newer EVs use NACS (J3400). For Level 2 charging, nearly every EV uses J1772, and Tesla cars use the J1772 adapter that came with the car.

    Why can't the map find my location?{{icon:chevron-down}}

    Your browser may have location access turned off for this site. Allow location in your browser settings, or type a city or ZIP code in the search box instead.
