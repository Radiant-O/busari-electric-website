Host a station

For shopping centers, restaurants, hotels, gas stations and parking operators.

EV drivers plan stops around fast chargers and spend time on site while they charge. Hosting a Busari Electric station puts your business on their map, and we can share charging revenue with you.

  • {{icon:check}}DC fast or Level 2 chargers, chosen for how long your visitors stay
  • {{icon:check}}Your station listed on the Busari Electric map and in car navigation apps
  • {{icon:check}}Ownership options from no upfront cost to revenue share

Fleet charging

For delivery vans, service trucks, shuttles and company cars.

We size depot charging to your routes and shifts, so every vehicle leaves full without paying for more power than you need.

  • {{icon:check}}Depot chargers with smart scheduling to cut peak demand charges
  • {{icon:check}}Fleet accounts for drivers charging on the public network
  • {{icon:check}}One monthly invoice with energy use by vehicle and driver

Workplace and multifamily charging

For offices, campuses, apartments and condominiums.

Level 2 chargers let employees and residents charge while they work or sleep. You set who can use them and what they pay.

  • {{icon:check}}Access control: private, shared or open to the public
  • {{icon:check}}Set your own pricing, or offer charging free as a benefit
  • {{icon:check}}Load management so chargers fit your building's existing power

From first call to first charge

We manage each step, including permits and the utility connection.

  1. Site assessment

    We review your parking, electrical service and visitor patterns, and recommend the right number and type of chargers.

  2. Proposal and incentives

    You get a fixed proposal with ownership options, plus the utility rebates and state incentives your site qualifies for.

  3. Permits and installation

    Licensed electricians install the chargers. We handle permits, inspections and coordination with your utility.

  4. Go live and stay running

    Your chargers go on the Busari Electric network. We monitor them around the clock and send technicians for repairs.
