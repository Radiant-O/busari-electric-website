How charging works

No app or membership needed. Here is a full charging session, start to finish.

  1. Find an open charger

    Our map shows which plugs are free right now and how far they are from you.

  2. Plug in

    Fast chargers have CCS1 and NACS cables. Level 2 chargers use J1772.

  3. Tap to pay

    Tap a credit or debit card, Apple Pay or Google Pay on the reader. Charging starts in seconds.

  4. Unplug and go

    Get your receipt by text or email. Move your car within {{price.idleGraceMinutes}} minutes of finishing to avoid idle fees.

Common questions

Will a Busari Electric charger work with my car?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Almost certainly. Our DC fast chargers have both CCS1 and NACS (J3400) cables, which together fit nearly every electric car sold in the US, including Tesla, Ford, GM, Hyundai, Kia, Rivian and BMW. Our Level 2 chargers use J1772; Tesla drivers can use the adapter that came with their car.

How much does it cost to charge?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Pay as you go costs {{price.dcFastPerKwh}} per kWh for DC fast charging and {{price.level2PerKwh}} per kWh for Level 2. Busari Plus members pay {{price.plusDcFastPerKwh}} per kWh for DC fast and {{price.plusLevel2PerKwh}} per kWh for Level 2, for {{price.plusMonthly}} a month. See pricing for details.

Do I need an app or account to charge?{{icon:chevron-down}}

No. Tap a credit or debit card, Apple Pay or Google Pay on the charger's reader to start. An account is only needed for Busari Plus member pricing.

How long does DC fast charging take?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Most electric cars charge from 10% to 80% in about 20 to 40 minutes on a DC fast charger. The exact time depends on your car's maximum charging speed, battery temperature and how full the battery is when you start.

How do I find the nearest available charger?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Open the Busari Electric map and choose "Use my location", or type a city or ZIP code. Stations are sorted by distance and show how many plugs are free right now.
