We arrange Level 1 and Level 2 charger installation for your house. Tell us about your home and we'll get back to you.

Charger types

Level 1

Uses a regular 120-volt wall outlet. Adds about 3 to 5 miles of range per hour. Good if you drive short distances.

Level 2

Uses a 240-volt circuit, like a dryer. Adds about 20 to 40 miles of range per hour, so most cars are full by morning.
