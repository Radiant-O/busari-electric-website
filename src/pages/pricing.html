Pricing and payment

Charging at our station uses {{partner.name}} pricing. We don't add any fees of our own.

Where to see the price

The price depends on your plan and how much energy your car takes. You can check it before you start: open the {{partner.name}} app and select our station on the map, or read it on the charger screen.
