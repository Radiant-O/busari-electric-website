Price questions

How much does it cost to charge at BusterElectric?{{icon:chevron-down}}

You pay the standard {{partner.name}} rate. We do not add any fee on top. The exact price shows in the {{partner.name}} app and on the charger screen before you start, and your total depends on how much energy your car takes.

Can I pay without downloading an app?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Yes. Tap or insert a credit or debit card on the charger. The app is optional.

Is there a fee for staying plugged in?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Idle fees may apply if your car stays plugged in after charging ends. Move your car when it is done so the next driver can charge.
