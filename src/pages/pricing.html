How billing works

  • {{icon:gauge}}

    You pay for energy, not time

    A kWh is a unit of energy. A typical EV goes 3 to 4 miles on one kWh, so a 40 kWh fast charge adds roughly 120 to 160 miles.

  • {{icon:credit-card}}

    A temporary hold, then the real total

    When you tap your card, your bank may show a temporary hold. It is replaced by the actual session total, usually within a few days.

  • {{icon:clock}}

    Idle fees keep chargers free

    If your car stays plugged in more than {{price.idleGraceMinutes}} minutes after charging stops, an idle fee of {{price.idlePerMinute}} a minute applies. You get a text before it starts.

  • {{icon:smartphone}}

    Receipts for every session

    Each receipt shows energy delivered, price per kWh, time and total, ready for expense reports.
