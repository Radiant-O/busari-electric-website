Short answer: BusterElectric has a DC fast charging station at 2404 South Collins Street, Arlington, TX 76014. It's open 24 hours a day, has CCS1 and NACS (Tesla) plugs, and you can pay with a credit card or the E-Fill Electric app.

Arlington has plenty of EV drivers and not nearly enough fast chargers. If you live here, commute through on I-20 or I-30, or you're in town for a game or a concert, this guide covers what you need to know before you plug in at our station.

Where is the station?

The station is at 2404 South Collins Street, Arlington, Texas 76014. Tap Get directions to open it in Google Maps, or see it on our map to check how far it is from you.

When is it open?

All day, every day. The chargers are available 24 hours, 7 days a week, so you can charge late at night or early in the morning.

Will it work with my car?

Almost certainly. The chargers have two kinds of cable:

  • NACS, the plug Tesla uses. Newer cars from other brands are switching to it too.
  • CCS1, used by most non-Tesla EVs sold in the US, including Ford, Chevrolet, Hyundai, Kia, BMW, Volkswagen, Audi, Rivian and many more.

If your car has either port, you don't need an adapter. Not sure which one you have? Read our guide to CCS1 vs. NACS.

How do I pay?

You have two options:

  1. Tap or insert a credit or debit card at the charger. No app or account needed.
  2. Use the E-Fill Electric app to start and pay from your phone.

The price shows in the app and on the charger screen before you start. We use standard E-Fill Electric rates and don't add any fees of our own. See pricing and payment for details.

How long will it take?

Most EVs go from about 10% to 80% in 20 to 45 minutes on a DC fast charger. Your car's top charging speed, its battery temperature and how full the battery already is all make a difference. We explain this in how long fast charging takes.

A few tips

  • Check live charger status in the E-Fill Electric app before you drive over.
  • Move your car when charging is done. Idle fees may apply, and the next driver will thank you.
  • If something isn't working, call charger support at {{site.phone}}.
